Frequently Asked Questions

What is Ember Connect?

Ember Connect is a video matching platform that allows users to instantly connect via secure video and voice calls. Users can watch rewarded ads to earn credits for free calls, or buy premium plans. Verified profiles can connect casually or offer their time as Earners.

How does the ad-reward system work?

Users can watch short video ads to earn 2 minutes of call credit. Credits are calculated to the exact second, meaning if your call ends early, any remaining seconds are preserved for future calls with the same person.

How do earners get paid?

Earners receive 60% of the revenue generated from subscription-based call minutes. Earnings accumulate in real-time and can be requested for payout via bank transfer or UPI once the minimum balance threshold is reached.

Is my personal data secure?

Absolutely. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, we store active consent logs server-side and protect verification uploads using short-lived signed URLs. You can withdraw your consent or request data erasure at any time from your profile settings.

Is it free to use?

Yes! Ember Connect is free to download and use. You can earn free call credits by watching short video ads. If you'd rather skip ads, call minute packs start from just ₹150.

How is verification done?

We use real-time selfie verification against your uploaded profile photos. Uploads are encrypted and stored temporarily using secure short-lived URLs, then automatically deleted after verification is complete.
