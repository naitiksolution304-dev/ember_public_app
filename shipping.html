Shipping & Delivery Policy

Version 2026-08-08 · Last updated 8 August 2026

1. Digital Products and Services

Ember Connect provides digital services, in-app minute recharges, and subscription plans accessible through our mobile application available on Android and iOS platforms. No physical goods or tangible items are shipped.

2. Instant Electronic Delivery

Upon successful payment completion via our authorized payment gateways (Razorpay), your purchased digital service, minute balance, or subscription benefits are delivered and credited to your registered Ember Connect account **instantly (within seconds)**.

3. Confirmation and Receipts

Upon successful transaction, an electronic confirmation receipt with transaction details is sent immediately to your registered email address and displayed in your app transaction history.

4. Delivery Issues & Support

If you experience any delay or issue with digital credit delivery after payment confirmation, please contact our support team immediately at support@emberconnect.in or Contact Us. We resolve all account credit queries within 24 hours.
